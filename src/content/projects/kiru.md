Kiru was the next game I undertook after Momentum Is Conserved. Kiru is a roguelike where strokes on the screen get translated into attacks. I created models, environments, and animations with Blender, as well as built and trained the stroke recognition model with TensorFlow.

While I never really got close to releasing Kiru, I still think it's a really interesting project. If I'm ever super free, I'll definitely come back to finish and release Kiru. Also, continuing it would allow me the opportunity to continue development on it.
