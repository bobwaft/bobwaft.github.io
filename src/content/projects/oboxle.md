Oboxle was my StormHacks 2026 submission and my second ever hackathon project. Oboxle is a browser based game that translates your movements through your webcam into boxing moves, allowing you to digitally box your friends. I worked on the 3D UI (just like this website), and made the avatar scanner.

StormHacks took place the week after HelloHacks, so we went into this project freshly inspired after seeing our competitors' projects. I'm particularly proud of how the UI looked for this one, and although we didn't win anything I'm really proud of how this project ended up, and in the future I want to experiment more with UI like this.
