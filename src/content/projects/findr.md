Findr was my first ever hackathon project and my submission to HelloHacks 2026, a hackathon hosted by UBC Biztech in September. Findr is a webapp that scrapes the Instagram posts of clubs at UBC and compiles event listings. I worked on the webscraper, as well as the Gemini pipeline.

As my first hackathon project, Findr gave me really valuable experience vis-a-vis working on projects in a group setting, pitching ideas, and understanding what goes into an app apart from software. I've also continued doing hackathons with the same group, and HelloHacks in general let me meet a lot of people at UBC.
