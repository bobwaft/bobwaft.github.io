Microwave AI was a course project where I headed a team of 3 to build a webapp that predicts microwave cook times from images of food. I used TensorFlow in Python to create the prediction model, and I also organized our development milestones and the presentations for the team.

Microwave AI actually has a really special place in my heart because it allowed me to get really close with my CS teachers in high school, and it was my first real project in a group setting. The initial pitch for Microwave AI actually took place outside of the context of the classroom, and I even ended up writing my personal statement for college applications about Microwave AI.
